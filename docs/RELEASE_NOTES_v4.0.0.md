## 🌟 Executive Overview
WorkHub ERP Release `v4.0.0` introduces the **Unified CRM V3 Commercial Engine** and the **Executive Management Information System (MIS Cockpit)** with 100% deterministic mathematical auditability. 

This release establishes an unbroken financial and operational chain connecting **Sales & CRM** $\rightarrow$ **Commercial Contracts (OA)** $\rightarrow$ **Tax Invoicing** $\rightarrow$ **Payment Collections** $\rightarrow$ **Loaded Direct Labor Costs** $\rightarrow$ **Itemized P&L Ledger Drill-Downs**.

---

## 🚀 Key Feature Highlights

### 1. Unified CRM V3 Commercial Engine
* **Complete Quotation-to-Cash Chain**: Seamless lifecycle progression from Quotation (with Win-Rate & Discount analytics) $\rightarrow$ Order Acknowledgement (OA) $\rightarrow$ Tax Invoices $\rightarrow$ Bank Collections.
* **Opportunity & Pipeline Tracking (MIS-06)**: Multi-stage pipeline analytics with weighted probability forecasting and sales touchpoints.
* **Quotation Conversion Ledger (C-02)**: Real-time calculation of quote acceptance rates, discount deviations, and commercial turnaround time.

### 2. Executive Management Information System (MIS Cockpit)
* **Monthly Business Review (MBR / MIS-04)**: Real-time consolidation of Invoiced Revenue, Collections, Loaded Payroll Costs, Operational Opex, Capex, and Net Operating Margin.
* **Multi-Source Client Profitability (MIS-02)**: Reconciles revenue across commercial tax invoices, active contracts, payment journals, and client accounting ledgers to accurately compute gross margins for domestic and imported client accounts.

### 3. Transparent Ledger Drill-Downs & Universal Record Inspection
* **Itemized Drill-Down Drawers**: Clicking any executive KPI card or summary row instantly displays every underlying transaction line item (invoices, salary slips, expense receipts) directly from the tenant database.
* **Record Details Slide-Over**: Universal inspector drawer available across all domain data grids (Daily Attendance, Payroll Statutory, Tasks Velocity, IT Assets, CRM Pipeline).

### 4. 100% Deterministic Mathematical Precision
* **Zero Approximated Figures**: Attendance rates, Ticket SLA compliance, and Payroll execution coverage ($X/N$ staff processed) calculate directly from active records.
* **Baseline Period Intelligence**: Zero-baseline comparisons render clear, professional `Baseline` indicators rather than mathematically misleading $+100.0\%$ variances.
